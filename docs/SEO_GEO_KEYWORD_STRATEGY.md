# 247ROI SEO/GEO Keyword Strategy

Last updated: 2026-08-01

## Strategic Bet

247ROI should not chase only today’s obvious search volume. The better 6-12 month bet is to own the emerging language around managed AI employees and AI workflow automation for service businesses.

The target buyer will not always search “AI employee” yet. They may search around pains, roles, and outcomes:

- slow lead response
- missed calls
- estimate follow-up
- contractor bid intake
- AI estimator assistant
- AI receptionist for contractors
- workflow automation for service businesses
- AI agents for small business
- custom AI automation agency

The content system should build topical authority around one clear market position:

> 247ROI builds managed AI employees and workflow automation systems for service businesses.

## Recommendation

Focus on a select number of clusters, then publish supporting pages and videos around those clusters consistently.

Do not mass-publish thin pages. Publish a controlled number of high-intent, answer-style assets with specific workflow examples, buyer pains, implementation details, human approval rules, and clear next steps.

Suggested cadence once the content pipeline exists:

- 2-4 high-intent web pages per month
- 4-8 short-form videos per month derived from those pages
- 1 deeper comparison or pillar page per month
- Refresh top pages every 60-90 days based on Search Console and LLM test results

## Terms to Deprioritize

These can appear naturally, but they should not be the main strategy:

- generic AI chatbot
- chatbot for small business
- AI tool
- virtual assistant
- AI voice bot only
- generic automation software
- AI SaaS

Reason:

They are either too broad, too commoditized, or they pull 247ROI toward a weaker category. 247ROI should be seen as a managed AI employee/workflow automation company, not another chatbot or tool subscription.

## Content Pipeline Rules

Every generated web page should have:

- one primary keyword
- one clear buyer
- one specific workflow
- one concrete pain
- one recommended first build
- human approval rules
- useful outputs
- FAQ section
- internal links to `/hire`, `/ai-employees`, `/demo`, and the most relevant related pages
- schema where appropriate

Every generated video concept should have:

- one target phrase
- one hook based on a real operational pain
- one example workflow
- one practical takeaway
- one soft CTA to the AI Employee Audit

Avoid:

- publishing many near-duplicate pages
- swapping city/trade names without real specificity
- claiming every build has direct ROI
- overusing “scorecard”
- thin AI-generated posts with no operational insight
- pages that compete with the primary `/hire` conversion path

## Working Verdict

The current direction is the right one:

- Own “AI employees” before it becomes a mature paid-search battlefield.
- Use “AI workflow automation agency” to capture current market demand.
- Use role-specific pages to catch high-intent operational searches.
- Use contractor/service-business pages to make the positioning concrete.
- Use LLM files, schema, FAQs, About/entity content, and internal links to make the site easy for AI systems to understand.

The next move should be a controlled content engine, not an uncontrolled page factory.
