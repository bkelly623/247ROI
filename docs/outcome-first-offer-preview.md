## Research and commercial recommendation
The sources establish supported/promoted HighLevel use cases, NOT a ranked market-share list or proof of easier sales for 247ROI. Ignore vendor/affiliate conversion figures without usable supporting evidence.

1. Lead with missed-call recovery for phone-led service businesses that actually miss inquiries. It is a concrete, demonstrable first problem with a bounded setup. Discovery opener: “When you're on a job and can't answer, what happens to that caller?” If that is not a problem, do not force the pitch.
2. Main service behind the opener: lead response and follow-up through to booking. Include website inquiries, assignment/human handoff and appropriately stopped follow-up—not just an automatic apology text. This gives more business value than selling a platform toggle.
3. AI receptionist is an alternative for after-hours/overflow coverage and routine booking, not automatically stacked on every missed-call flow. Needs approved knowledge, calendar rules, escalation and more testing.
4. Booking confirmations/reminders/rescheduling belong in the visible offer set. These extend the inquiry-to-appointment journey and are directly supported by GHL.
5. Review requests are a clear add-on/alternative where existing customer volume supports it. Say automated review REQUESTS, not automated reviews or guaranteed five-star reviews. Invite honest feedback without rating-based gating or incentives.
6. Past-customer reactivation is a secondary, discovery-led offer: useful records and permission are prerequisites. Do not lead with bulk texting an old list, generic funnels, social posting, or a sprawling all-in-one platform pitch.

Measure qualified conversations, paid starts, retained clients, delivery/support effort and attributable bookings—not a hypothetical popular-service ranking. A first-call pitch should offer a short demonstration after identifying a real gap, not list every service.

## Demonstration plan
Use ONE explicitly fictional GHL demo sub-account/business with a dedicated test number, sample website form, test calendar and designated consenting test contacts. It supports:
- Missed call -> SMS -> reply in conversation inbox -> owner handoff.
- Inquiry form -> acknowledgment -> routing/follow-up -> test booking.
- AI receptionist -> routine query -> test appointment or handoff. Disclose AI; no invented business answers.
- Booking -> confirmation -> accelerated TEST reminder -> cancellation/reschedule behavior.
- Completed test job -> review-request message -> clearly fake review destination. NEVER solicit or post a fake Google review for a demo.
Record short walkthroughs of those VERIFIED flows; offer controlled live number/form testing where safe. Use consent, opt-out, repeat-caller suppression and usage controls. Confirm number/SMS eligibility and registration before activation. Never route unsolicited messages to arbitrary demo-entered numbers.

This preview instead contains five locally interactive, scripted illustrations, prominently labeled fictional/simulated. They send no calls/messages, book nothing and post no reviews. They are not proof of GHL delivery. Existing 3001 custom Twilio implementation is neither migrated nor relabeled as a GHL demo.
